# Project shitfan (working title)

I want to create a document aimed at a hypothetical, but entirely feasable future situation.
The goal of the document is to inform people of important facts and information in case of
emergencies or disasters. It is not a general prepping guide (such as how to make a fire etc.), 
but an extensive resource specifically for a particular region or country. Use all available 
examples and best practices currently online.

## Basic question
The basic question is: "What are the plans and preparations of my national government or local organisation(s) in case of major disaster/emergencies and what to do, where to go etc. and any of a number of plausible scenarios (war, epidemics, etc.) are happening and this document is all you have in case all online information is unavailable."

## Base assumption
The assumption is that a disaster or emergency has taken place *and* the Internet is no longer
available, so important online information on what to do or where to go is no longer available.
Users will either use a paper version or digital version in an offline device, such as project NOMAD or Ktemata (ktemata.org).

## Output formats
Output should be in Markdown, PDF, zim (for Kiwix), and future physical paper  or book.
The Markdown file is leading and the single source of truth.
